How it works

Up and running in a minute

Nothing to install on the phone and nothing to sign up for.

  1. Open CamShare on your PC

    Choose the screen you want to record. A QR code appears for your phone.

  2. Scan the code with your phone

    The camera page opens in the phone's browser. Tap Connect Camera.

  3. Place the camera and record

    Drag and resize the camera on the preview, then press Record. The video is saved on your PC.

Features

Everything you need for a clear walkthrough

Tutorials, bug reports, demos and lessons, with your face where it helps and out of the way where it doesn't.

Move and resize the camera

Drag the camera anywhere on the preview and resize it, before or during the recording.

Your phone is the webcam

Use the phone's front or back camera, and flip between them while you record.

Smooth 30 fps, up to 1080p

Recorded at 30 frames per second, up to 1080p. Smaller screens are never upscaled.

Audio only when you want it

Recordings are silent by default. Tick one box to add your PC microphone. System sound and the phone's mic are never recorded.

Files that play and seek

Saved as WebM with the length and a seek index written in, so you can jump around in Windows Media Player.

No account, no sign-up

Install it, scan the code, record. There's nothing to log in to, on the PC or on the phone.

Privacy

Your recordings stay on your PC

  • Saved locallyVideos are written to your Videos\CamShare folder, or a folder you choose. CamShare never uploads them.
  • Encrypted camera linkThe phone sends its camera over an encrypted WebRTC connection. Our server only helps the two devices find each other and never receives your video.
  • Camera only on the phoneThe phone page asks for the camera, never the microphone.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warns about it.

To continue, click More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for the file you downloaded from this page or from the CamShare GitHub releases.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

CamShare has been tested on Android, including switching between the front and back cameras.

iPhone hasn't been tested yet. The phone page uses standard browser camera features, but we can't promise it works on iPhone until it's been checked.

Do the phone and PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

It's recommended. Both devices need internet access to pair. Once paired, the camera video goes straight from the phone to the PC, which is most reliable when both are on the same network.

Keep the camera page open and in the foreground on the phone while you record.

What format are the videos saved in?

Recordings are normally saved as WebM files named like CamShare-20261119-143005.webm. CamShare adds the duration and a seek index when it saves, so Windows Media Player shows the length and lets you jump around.

Existing files are never overwritten.

Is sound recorded?

Not unless you ask for it. Audio is off by default. Turn on Record PC microphone to record your Windows default microphone. Windows system sound and the phone's microphone are never recorded.

The first connection is slow. Why?

The pairing server goes to sleep when nobody is using it. If it was idle, the first connection can take around 30 seconds while it wakes up. CamShare shows a message while it waits.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. CamShare currently runs on Windows only.
